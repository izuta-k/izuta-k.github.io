In my last French post, I mentioned that my French writing skills are very limited. I can write and read texts without any problem (enough to obtain a bachelor's degree entirely in French), but I want to become more efficient and improve my ability to structure complex ideas more simply and clearly in French. Without practice, this isn't possible, so I want to write more in french. But my french have a mistake I cant shake off: Anglicisms. French is my second foreign language. I've already absorbed English, and all the grammatical structures are reflected in french sentences I form. This is an acceptable habit for someone at B1 or B2 level, but my goal is to be able to form clearer and simple sentences in french. So I need to practice more.

Due to the accumulation of a lot of uncertainties this summer, I'm slowly becoming more depressed and lacking in energy. But if I want to become a good researcher, I need to recalibrate my habits when they go off track. Now, I've received my degree and my letter of acceptance to INRS. Even though I don't know when (or even if) I will get my study permit, I need to develop the skills necessary to succeed in my master's degree and make it less stressful while I am there.

That is why I will start with putting smaller objectives about what I want to do in my masters. 
1) Be able to create, manipulate and then use large datasets.
2) Do statistical analyses with these datasets.
3) Not have problems with the french I am about to face.
4) Have a substantial base material read and processed before I go.

Each one of these goals are quite achieveable in around 1 months of consistent work if I believe correctly. Structuring each of these into much smaller goals and spreading around the 4 months I have in front of me is a tad bit hard though.

To be honest the first 3 goals are all about improving what I already have. But the last one has a substantial workload since I can't hope to achieve it by reading just a couple of books. I think I will start with the most easy to read and classic literature sources about the courses and the thesis I plan to take and write. 1 month will be curated AI list but I will put more rigorously found studies on top of it.

I hope to write more in this 4 months.
